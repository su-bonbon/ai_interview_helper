Free U.S. Citizenship Interview Prep

US Citizenship Prep is a free bilingual study resource for people preparing for the naturalization interview. The site includes civics practice, N-400 interview preparation, reading and writing guidance, mock interview prompts, and interview-day checklists.

What applicants can study here

The citizenship interview is not only a memory test. Applicants may need to answer civics questions, read and write simple English sentences, review the N-400 application, explain updates since filing, and show documents when a topic needs proof. This site organizes those tasks into practical study pages so applicants can prepare one step at a time.

  • N-400 interview questions
  • U.S. citizenship mock interview practice
  • Citizenship test practice
  • Citizenship test practice in Spanish and English
  • Citizenship interview checklist

Original preparation guidance

Our guides are written around real preparation tasks rather than generic keyword summaries. Applicants can learn how to study civics questions in small groups, how to practice reading and writing without panic, how to review travel and work history, and how to organize documents for interview day. We also explain that this site is educational and not legal advice.

For official rules, forms, and eligibility details, applicants should verify information with USCIS. We maintain a separate sources page that links to official USCIS resources and explains how our educational content is created and reviewed.
